Good to Know

Arizona mortgage FAQs

Do you do home loans in Arizona?

Yes. I'm licensed in Arizona and work with buyers and homeowners across the state — the Phoenix metro, Tucson, Flagstaff, and everywhere in between — for purchases and refinances, all handled securely online or by phone.

Can you help with a second home or winter home in Arizona?

Absolutely — second homes are a big part of the Arizona market. Financing a second home works a little differently than a primary residence, and I'll walk you through how down payment and qualifying differ before you commit to anything.

Do you work with Arizona real estate investors?

Yes. DSCR loans let investors qualify on a property's expected rental income rather than personal income, and bank statement programs help self-employed buyers. I'll help you compare those against conventional investment-property financing.

I'm relocating to Arizona — can you help?

Definitely. Relocations are a big part of what I do, and I'm licensed in Arizona, Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Texas, and Wyoming — so I can help whether you're moving to Arizona or from it.
