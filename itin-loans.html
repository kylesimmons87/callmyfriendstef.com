ITIN Loans

“Homeownership without a Social Security number.”

ITIN loans let borrowers who file taxes with an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) instead of a Social Security number pursue homeownership. They're a non-QM option, and requirements vary by lender and by the states where the program is available.

Who it may be a good fit for

  • Borrowers who file taxes with an ITIN rather than an SSN
  • Buyers who have steady, documentable income but no Social Security number
  • Families ready to buy who thought they didn't have options

Key things to know

  • Down payment, reserve, and rate requirements vary by lender
  • Availability can vary by licensed state
  • Documentation expectations differ from a standard loan

What you'll typically need

  • Your ITIN and tax filing history
  • Income and employment documentation
  • Bank and asset statements
